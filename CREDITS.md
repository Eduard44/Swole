# Credits

Third-party material bundled with Swole.

## Exercise data

- **free-exercise-db** by yuhonas (https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db), version of 27 September 2026 (commit f00c92c). Released into the public domain under the Unlicense. Swole keeps the name, category, equipment, level, muscles and instructions of each exercise; images are not included. Muscle names are mapped onto Swole's own groups in `src/exercises/mapping.ts`.
