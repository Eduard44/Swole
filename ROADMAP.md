# Roadmap

The build order. One step at a time, each one checkable on the live link.

- [x] 1. Repo setup: project scaffold, theme tokens, light and dark, deploy to Pages
- [x] 2. Body map: figures, tap to select, front/back and male/female toggle (first-draft figures, to refine with the owner)
- [x] 3. Exercise library: import dataset, list, search, filter (exercise data only, no images yet)
- [x] 4. Session logging and rest timer (with your own exercises; weights are in kg until the units setting in step 7)
- [ ] 5. History
- [ ] 6. Recovery rules and "what to train today"
- [ ] 7. Settings, backup, PWA install and offline check
- [ ] 8. Polish pass on motion and details

## Waiting on the owner

- Feedback on the figures: muscle shapes, proportions, the female figure.
- Exercise images. The dataset's photos total about 105 MB, too much to ship inside a phone app, and loading them from the internet would break "no network calls". Options: no images, a small hand-picked set, or Swole's own pencil sketches for common exercises.
- Recovery colors on the body map (proposal: shades of violet, since red means "selected"). Needed in step 6.
- Recovery rule numbers (hours until a muscle counts as fresh). Needed in step 6.
